# Section-head photography — sources and licence

Decorative photography behind the `/website` section headings
(`.ws-head--media` / `SECTION_HEAD_MEDIA` in
`src/features/website/WebsitePage.js`).

Every file here is from **StockSnap.io**, published under
**Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**: free for commercial
use, no attribution required, no model/property release needed for the uses
StockSnap allows. Credits are recorded anyway so the source can be re-checked.

| File | Section | StockSnap photo ID | Subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cars.webp` | Our Cars | `KEPSFKGIL9` | Headlight detail on a silver car (no visible badge) |
| `offers.webp` | Latest Offers | `F2QIF4C8DI` | Hand holding out a car key |
| `shop.webp` | Shop | `4RHOB6RC3S` | Chrome sockets on a red toolbox |

Source URL pattern: `https://cdn.stocksnap.io/img-thumbs/960w/<ID>.jpg`
(landing page: `https://stocksnap.io/photo/<ID>`).

Originals were resized to 900px wide and re-encoded to WebP (quality 74).

**When adding a new one:** use a public-domain / CC0 source, avoid visible
manufacturer badges and number plates (Humphries & Parks sells Suzuki, KGM and
Mitsubishi — a rival badge in the heading reads as a mistake), and add a row
here.
