# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HNPSystem is used by dealership staff across workshop, service, parts, accounts, HR, management and technician roles. Users work in operational environments where they need to understand current state quickly, complete high-frequency tasks accurately and move between related records without losing context.

## Product Purpose

HNPSystem is Humphries & Parks' dealer management system. It brings day-to-day workshop, vehicle, customer, stock, purchasing, finance, people and reporting workflows into one role-aware application. Success means staff can see what needs attention, make the correct operational decision and complete the next action with minimal delay or ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy and composed. The interface should feel like a dependable workshop tool: compact enough for operational use, calm under pressure and explicit about state, risk and required action.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, low-contrast grey-on-tint text, novelty navigation, generic card walls, gratuitous animation and styling that competes with operational data. Do not imitate consumer shopping interfaces or separate consumables from their established workshop workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next operational decision in view before secondary analysis.
2. Preserve one source of truth for roles, data, statuses and workflows.
3. Use familiar, consistent controls so the interface disappears into the task.
4. Communicate state redundantly through clear language and accessible semantic colour.
5. Keep dense information legible across workshop desktops, tablets and phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for staff interfaces. Body and control text must maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast in every supported theme; large text and non-text state indicators must maintain at least 3:1. Keyboard focus, 44 by 44 pixel touch targets, zoom and text reflow, reduced motion and colour-independent status communication are required.
